My art

Beyond finance

Outside of class and work, I make digital illustrations and produce audio. I'm in the Rutgers Animation Club, and I treat art as a skill I keep working on, not a hobby I already finished learning.

How I make a piece

  1. Collect ideas and referencesI start with a rough idea and gather reference images so I'm drawing from something real, not guessing.
  2. Sketch smallI try several quick thumbnail sketches and pick the one with the strongest shape and mood.
  3. Build it up digitallyI clean up the line work, block in color, then add light, shadow, and detail.
  4. Step away and critiqueI come back with fresh eyes, note what feels off, and revise before calling it done.

How I'm getting better

  • Practicing fundamentals like shape, color, and lighting on a regular basis
  • Studying work by artists I admire and working out why it succeeds
  • Getting feedback from other artists in the Rutgers Animation Club
  • Comparing early pieces with new ones to see what has improved
